  9. 5

    Recommendations

    Quick wins the contractor could act on straight away.

What happened next

Willmott Dixon adopted 3 of our 5 recommendations:

  • Icons on signs for workers with little English
  • A translated glossary of common site words
  • Easier-to-read minutes from worker meetings

Multiplex audited all its dry-lining subcontractors.
