Shapes and spacing

Lines are always 2px ink, straight or wavy. Buttons are pills, at least 44px tall. Spacing steps: 4, 8, 14, 18, 22, 28, 40. Phone gutter 20px. Card padding 18px. Sticky top bar 48px.

Image style

Photos run full colour inside a 2px ink frame with rounded corners. Project photos start grey under a hand-drawn cover (ink lines over a 45% paper wash). The cover slides off in step with the scroll and the photo turns to colour. Reduced motion: no covers. Graphics use the same ink, paper, lines and type, never a new colour.
